Privacy Policy

Elevated Design Co · Last updated November 24, 2026

Elevated Design Co runs two small private tools for its own shop: EDC Engine alerts (Google) and Elevated Design Co Pin Scheduler (Pinterest). They are used only by the owner of Elevated Design Co. They are not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to them.

EDC Engine alerts (Google)

With the owner's permission, it uses two Google permissions on the shop's own Google account:

  • Send email — to send the owner short shop alerts and reminders.
  • Calendar events — to add reminders to the owner's own calendar.

It does not read the inbox, contacts or existing calendar entries.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Elevated Design Co Pin Scheduler (Pinterest)

It connects only to the shop's own Pinterest business account (elevateddesignstudio), using Pinterest's official sign-in (OAuth). It never asks for or stores a Pinterest password. It uses that access to:

  • publish Pins of the shop's own product images, each linking to the matching Etsy listing;
  • read the shop's own boards, Pins and Pin analytics to see which designs perform best.

It does not access, collect or store any other Pinterest user's data, and it follows Pinterest's Developer Guidelines, rate limits and AI content labeling.

What we store and share

We keep only the sign-in keys Google and Pinterest provide, stored encrypted, plus a log of the shop's own posts and their analytics. We do not sell, share or transfer this data to anyone, and do not use it for advertising.

Removing access

Google access can be removed at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Pinterest access can be removed from Pinterest Settings → Security and logins → Apps. Stored keys are deleted on request.
