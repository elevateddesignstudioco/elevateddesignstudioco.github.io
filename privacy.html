Privacy Policy

Elevated Design Co · EDC Engine alerts · Last updated November 21, 2026

What this app is

EDC Engine alerts is a private tool used only by the owner of Elevated Design Co. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses two Google permissions on the owner's own account:

  • Send email — to send the owner short shop alerts and reminders.
  • Calendar events — to add reminders to the owner's own calendar.

It does not read your inbox, contacts or existing calendar entries.

What it stores and shares

It keeps only the sign-in key Google provides, stored encrypted, so alerts can keep working. It does not sell, share or transfer any Google data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
